# Why the rule on the backlog exists

Magnus, 2026-11-27, deferring the design of a formatting bar above selected text: "Please put this in our backlog and
make sure that if it was not a known document before it is now by adding references to it to the rules files."

Before then the project had no backlog: what was deferred lived only in the topics and the transcripts of the sessions
it was discussed in. The rule is what makes the document known to every session; without it, a session would neither
look in it nor add to it.
